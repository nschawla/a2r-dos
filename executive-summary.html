01 Executive Summary

A governed system of record for the entire delivery portfolio

A2R Delivery OS™ ("A2R DOS") is an enterprise-grade Delivery Operating System purpose-built for professional services organizations. It unifies commercial baselining, financial realization, resourcing, schedule governance, and risk management for an entire portfolio of client engagements into one governed, role-aware workspace — replacing the spreadsheets, point-in-time status decks, and disconnected timesheet tools most delivery organizations still stitch together by hand.

Every screen in A2R DOS — the Portfolio Control Tower, the Resource & Capacity Cockpit, Financial Realization, the RAID Cockpit, and the SteerCo and Executive Hub briefings — is driven from the same operational data the delivery organization already produces each week, and is automatically scoped to the person viewing it: a Practice Director and a VP see the identical screen, narrowed to exactly what their role should see.

This release, v1.4.0, advances three capabilities requested directly by the platform's largest engagements: a complete four-pillar weekly intake engine, role-based scoped filtering enforced at the data layer, and a Custom KPI Definition Engine that lets an administrator stand up a governed metric card without engineering involvement. All three ship alongside the platform's existing enterprise governance framework, SAML/OIDC identity federation, and SOC 2-aligned, hash-chained compliance ledger.

02 Core Platform Capabilities

Three capabilities delivered this release

Each capability below is live in production today, gated by the platform's existing role and governance model — no parallel access system, no new persona concept.

4-Pillar Weekly Ingestion Engine

One self-service portal (/admin/ingestion) now covers every weekly operational data stream a delivery organization files, with a shared quarantine-and-correct workflow: malformed rows are isolated with a plain-English reason and never silently dropped or committed.

Weekly ActualsHours by person & project
Milestone & ProgressPhase status, % complete
Forecast & EACRevised cost-to-complete, by role
Status & RAID LogNarrative highlight + RAID items

Role-Based Scoped Filtering

The same Control Tower, Capacity Cockpit, and Financial Realization screens now enforce visibility at the query layer, not just in navigation — a Practice Director is scoped strictly to their own practiceId, automatically.

VP / PMO / PS OpsGlobal every practice, every engagement
Practice DirectorScoped own practice only
Delivery ManagerScoped direct reports only
Project ManagerScoped own assignments only

Custom KPI Definition Engine

At /admin/kpis, an administrator binds a real metric — never an open formula language — to a target, a warning line, and the personas who should see it. The card appears on the Control Tower and Executive Hub immediately, with no redeploy.

  • Financial Realization — e.g. Blended Baseline Margin %
  • Schedule & Milestones — e.g. On-Track Phase %
  • RAID Cockpit — e.g. Open Critical RAID Items
  • Resource & Capacity — e.g. Blended Billable Utilization %
