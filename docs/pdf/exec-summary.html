01The problem we solve

Professional-services delivery leaders are forced into a false choice.

Spreadsheets and slideware are fast to start but fragile, un-auditable, and impossible to trust at portfolio scale. Numbers drift, versions fork, and there is no record of who changed what. General-purpose PM and ERP platforms either bolt "security" onto a UI layer that a determined user can walk around, or hand operators raw database and infrastructure access and call it power-user mode.

Neither gives a sponsor operational certainty — a single, current, tamper-evident view of engagement health, financial realization, and governance — without also widening the attack surface.

The A2R position

A2R-DOS removes the choice. It delivers the portfolio, financial, and governance model of a purpose-built PS platform on an architecture that enforces tenant isolation, auditability, and least-privilege operator access where they cannot be bypassed — at the database and the edge.

03Operational certainty & frictionless visibility

Certainty for the operator

One current source of truth. Every currency and rate figure is computed in exact decimal and rounded once, at its accounting boundary — the number on screen is the number that is stored. A locked baseline is the immutable reference every future comparison measures against.

Visibility for the stakeholder

A dedicated read-only Viewer tier: whole-org read of the portfolio and the SteerCo board, zero edit authority, every financial figure scrubbed server-side. Guests and clients see the picture without ever touching it, and without a bespoke export.

Governance is configurable per tenant — compliance templates toggle module visibility and financial masking — so the same platform fits a board-reporting engagement and a hands-on delivery team without a fork.

04Security posture at a glance

A2R-DOS reached v1.16.0 through seven successive audit-and-hardening rounds. The full architecture is documented separately in the Multi-Tenant Security Briefing; in summary:

  • Tenant isolation is enforced at four independent layers, including PostgreSQL row-level security — one client's data is never one bug away from another's.
  • Cryptographic key separation — the MFA secret is sealed under a dedicated, versioned key, fully decoupled from the session-signing secret, with zero-downtime rotation.
  • Atomic replay protection — second-factor consumption is a single conditional database write plus a row lock; a replayed code can never yield a second privileged session.
  • Three-layer operator RBAC across six least-privilege tiers, plus isolated guest observers — enforced at the edge, the page, and the action.
  • Engine-level audit immutability — the hash-chained ledger cannot be altered even by the application's own database role.

Compliance alignment

A2R-DOS is designed and operated in alignment with SOC 1 and SOC 2 control objectives — logical access control, change management, segregation of duties, and a tamper-evident audit trail. This is control-design alignment demonstrated by the repository's own evidence artifacts; no third-party SOC attestation has been performed, and this document claims none.
